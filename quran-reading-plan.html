Quran

Quran reading plan and khatam tracker

Lunora’s reading plans divide the Quran into daily portions and track your progress day by day, from a 30-day Ramadan khatam (one juz a day) to a khatam in 60 days or in a year. You can also build a plan from the surahs you choose, and pause a plan during your period if you wish. Lunora is an Islamic app for Muslim women, free to download on iPhone and Android.

The plans

Ten ready-made plans, or your own

Three khatam plans for reading the whole Quran, seven shorter plans, and a custom plan built from the surahs you pick.

Ramadan khatam

30 days, one juz a day, each day starting from the first verse of its juz.

Khatam in 60 days

The whole Quran in two months: about ten pages of the Madinah mushaf a day, with the page numbers for each day.

Khatam in a year

One to two pages a day for 365 days. A little each day, without stopping.

Seven short plans

For beginners (Al-Fatiha, then short surahs), Friday surahs, Morning surahs, Night surahs, Healing verses, Calming surahs and 7 surahs in 7 days.

Your own plan

Choose the surahs, give the plan a name and an icon if you like, then read one or two surahs a day or set the number of days yourself.

Your choice during your period

When Period Mode is on, you decide whether to pause or continue. A paused plan doesn’t move its day count and continues from the same day.

How it works

A plan that tells you what to read today

  1. 1

    Pick a plan

    Open Reading plans in Lunora and start a khatam plan, a short plan, or create your own.

  2. 2

    See today’s reading

    Every day shows exactly what to read: a page range, a juz or a surah. The whole plan is listed day by day.

  3. 3

    Read in the Quran reader

    Read as a list with translation and transliteration, or as mushaf pages with the Madinah mushaf layout. When you finish, tap “I’ve read it”.

  4. 4

    Track your progress

    The plan shows which day you are on and what percentage is done. If you switch to another plan, your progress is kept, and you can go back to it any time.

  5. 5

    Complete it

    On the last day Lunora marks the plan as completed, with “May Allah accept it”. You can start it again or choose another plan.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does Lunora have a khatam (hatim) tracker?

Yes. Lunora has three khatam plans: Ramadan khatam (30 days, one juz a day), khatam in 60 days (about ten pages a day) and khatam in a year (one to two pages a day). The app shows which day of the plan you are on and how much of it is done.

Can I make my own Quran reading plan?

Yes. Choose the surahs you want, add a name and an icon if you like, then read one surah a day, two a day, or set the number of days yourself. A day may then hold more than one surah.

How do I mark a day as read?

Open the day's reading from the plan, read it in Lunora's Quran reader and tap “I've read it”. The day is marked as done and the plan moves on to the next one.

What happens to my reading plan during my period?

If Period Mode is on, Lunora leaves the choice to you: pause the plan or continue. While it is paused the day count does not advance, and it carries on from the same day when you turn Period Mode off. The plan also links to the Period Mode guide, which lists the views of the four madhhabs on reading the Quran during menstruation.

Which mushaf layout does Lunora use?

The page view follows the page layout of the Madinah mushaf, 604 pages, and the 60-day and one-year khatam plans give their daily readings as page ranges of that mushaf. Translation and transliteration are available in list view.

Is Lunora available on iPhone and Android?

Lunora is free to download from the App Store and Google Play and has no ads. Lunora+ is a subscription that unlocks the full app, and you can start it with a free trial.
