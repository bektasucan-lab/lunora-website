Lunora is an Islamic app for Muslim women, available on iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play). This policy explains what stays on your device, what the app sends and why.

In short. What you write about yourself in Lunora (your name, your period, symptoms, notes, duas) stays on your device. To work and to get better, the app sends a small set of data described below. It is not tied to your name, email or an account: Lunora has no accounts.

1. Stays on your device

Your name and profile photo, period dates, symptoms and moods, journal and notes, your own duas, dhikr and prayer marks, missed-prayer counts, reading progress, settings, and the recitations you record in Memorize the Quran. Period data is kept in encrypted storage. We never receive the dates you enter or your symptoms.

2. What the app sends, and why

  • Approximate location, for prayer times. Your position rounded to two decimals (about 1 km), or your city and country, goes to the Aladhan prayer-time service (aladhan.com). We do not track your location in the background.
  • How widely features are used. To understand how widely Period Mode is used across all our users, we count when it is switched on or off, the same way we do for other features; in Luna, we count which feelings are picked most often. We do not send the dates you enter or your symptoms, and we do not know who you are.
  • Usage and diagnostics. Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, App Check) receives which screens and features are used, app language and country, app version, crash reports, and identifiers of the installation; on Android this may include the advertising ID. We use this to improve the app, to keep it secure, and to see whether our own ads led to an install. Lunora shows no ads and does not sell data.
  • Purchases. Apple or Google take the payment. RevenueCat keeps the subscription's status with an anonymous ID and an installation identifier.
  • What you choose to send us. If you report a translation or course problem, or answer "What held you back?", we receive your choice and the note if you write one, with your app language, version and platform; a report also says which text or lesson it is about, an answer also the plan you had selected and your store country. No name, account or device ID. Please do not write personal or health details. We cannot tie a note to you, so we cannot look one up for you; write to us and we will remove a note you can describe.
  • Downloads. Audio, library and Quran files come from media.mylunora.app (Cloudflare), everyayah.com and alquran.cloud. Like any web request, these servers see your IP address.
  • Email. If you write to support@mylunora.app we keep your message to answer you.

3. Permissions

Location (prayer times, qibla), notifications (adhan and reminders), microphone (only when you tap Record yourself; recordings stay on your device), camera and photos (profile photo, saving verse cards), app usage / Screen Time (only for Protect My Prayer; the list of apps stays on your device). You can refuse any of them.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. The services above receive only what they need for the purpose given.

5. Your choices and rights

You can delete entries one by one in the app, and uninstalling Lunora removes what it stored on your phone. Your phone's own iCloud or Google backup may keep a copy of app settings; that backup is yours and is not sent to us. To ask for access to, or deletion of, subscription or analytics records, write to support@mylunora.app. EU/UK users also have the right to complain to their data-protection authority.

6. Children

Lunora is not directed to children under 13.

7. Changes and contact

We will update the date above when this changes. Studio Lunora · support@mylunora.app
