Online tasbih counter

How to use it

  1. 1

    Pick a set

    After prayer, before sleep, istighfar, salawat, or a free count with no target.

  2. 2

    Tap or press space

    Each tap adds one. The ring fills as you go and moves on to the next phrase by itself.

  3. 3

    Finish and reset

    At the end you see your total for today. Reset starts the set again.

Where the numbers come from

After every prayer: the Prophet ﷺ taught saying SubhanAllah 33 times, Alhamdulillah 33 times and Allahu Akbar 33 times, then completing the hundred with “La ilaha illallah wahdahu la sharika lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamdu wa huwa ‘ala kulli shay’in qadir”. (Sahih Muslim 597)

Before sleep: he taught Ali and Fatimah to say SubhanAllah 33 times, Alhamdulillah 33 times and Allahu Akbar 34 times when going to bed, and told them it was better for them than a servant. (Sahih al-Bukhari 3113, Sahih Muslim 2727)

Questions

Is it fine to count dhikr with a digital counter?

Counting on the fingers is the Sunnah, and the Prophet ﷺ encouraged it. Many scholars see beads or a counter as a permissible tool for keeping count. If you are unsure, ask a scholar you trust.

Does the counter save my count?

Yes, on this device only. Your count and today’s total are stored in your browser. Nothing is sent to us, and there is no account.

Can I use it during my period?

Yes. Dhikr, dua and salawat are open to you during your period; the counter is a good companion for those days.

Is there a tasbih for my home screen?

Lunora has a tasbih widget for the iPhone and Android home screen, with a sourced dhikr library, so you can count without opening an app.

Keep your tasbih on your home screen

Lunora’s dhikr widget counts from your home screen, alongside prayer times, the Quran and Period Mode. No ads, in 9 languages.
